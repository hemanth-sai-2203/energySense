System Operator

Grid-India (POSOCO)

National System Operator under Electricity Act 2003 (NLDC + 5 Regional Load Despatch Centres).

  • Primary Stream: Real-time SCADA operational telemetry from substation transducers.
  • Publication: Daily Power Supply Position (PSP) reports at ~09:00 IST the following morning.
  • EnergySense Role: Powers Tab 1 & Tab 2 (National Demand) and Tab 5 (State Matrix).
  • Official Portal: grid-india.in • vidyutpravah.in
Technical Authority

Central Electricity Authority (CEA)

Statutory technical authority under Section 73 of the Electricity Act 2003.

  • Primary Stream: National power planning, coal stock tracking, Load Generation Balance Report (LGBR), monthly generation accounting.
  • Publication: Monthly generation reports & executive summaries.
  • EnergySense Role: Sourced via Ember's pipeline for Tab 4 (Fuel Mix to Nov 2025).
  • Official Portal: cea.nic.in • npp.gov.in
Economic Regulator

CERC

Quasi-judicial regulator under Section 76 of Electricity Act 2003.

  • Primary Stream: Interstate transmission tariffs, Indian Electricity Grid Code (IEGC), power exchange market monitoring (IEX, PXIL, HPX).
  • Crucial Distinction: CERC does not operate physical dispatch or stream daily telemetry feeds.
  • Official Portal: cercind.gov.in

Key Engineering Finding: SCADA Telemetry vs Commercial Settlement Accounts (REAs)

Grid-India reports instantaneous operational telemetry from SCADA transducers published on T+1. Regional Power Committees (RPCs) under CERC regulations publish Regional Energy Accounts (REAs) 2–4 weeks later based on certified Special Energy Meters (SEMs). Because of transducer calibration drift, unmetered auxiliary consumption, and transmission losses, SCADA daily MU figures typically diverge from final commercial accounts by ±0.5% to 2.5%.

📅 236 Missing Calendar Dates (93 Clusters)

The daily demand archive spans 4,136 elapsed calendar days between 2013-01-01 and 2024-04-28, but contains 3,900 observations. 236 days are missing, including an 82-day continuous gap in early 2013 (Jan 8 – Mar 30) and 50 days in 2020.

Why the Naive 8.27% CAGR Was Defective:
2013 only had 271 recorded days (716,800 MU) vs 364 in 2023 (1,586,383 MU). Comparing raw annual sums artificially deflated the 2013 baseline. Comparing normalized mean daily demand (2,645.0 MU/day in 2013 vs 4,358.2 MU/day in 2023) gives the true, defensible 5.12% CAGR.

⚠️ 6 Negative Peak Unmet Deficit Records

Under physical definitions, peak unmet deficit cannot be negative. However, the raw scraped `peak_unmet_MW.csv` contains 6 negative entries (down to -3,171 MW on 2014-07-23). These represent either transcription/OCR parsing errors in legacy PDF tables or misclassified surplus injections.

Data Governance Policy:
All raw negative values are strictly preserved in SQLite and the audit log (anomaly_flag = 'NEGATIVE_UNMET_DEFICIT'). Visual chart curves are clamped to 0 MW with explicit footnotes so as not to distort recruiter evaluation.

🌡️ ERA5 Climate Series Truncation (1,196 Dates)

The ECMWF Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI) series ends on 2020-12-31, leaving 1,196 daily rows (30.7%) between 2021 and 2024 unmerged.

Statistical Insight (R² = 0.0035):
The empirical Pearson correlation between national mean UTCI and daily demand across 2013–2020 is r = 0.059. Simultaneous summer heatwaves in North India and monsoon cooling in the South wash out national aggregate correlation, confirming climate cannot be used as a naive univariate linear predictor.

Q1: Can we get live feed or post-April 2024 data from official websites?

Yes. The data exists and is produced every single day by Grid-India. Grid-India publishes daily Power Supply Position reports every morning at ~09:00 IST in PDF format, and Vidyut Pravah provides 15-minute dispatch snapshots.

Why the April 28, 2024 cutoff? In scientific benchmarking, datasets are frozen at a published historical timestamp (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.14983362) to provide a standardized, reproducible testbed where models can be evaluated against verified ground truth without lookahead leakage. Note that Tab 4 (Fuel Mix) already extends into late 2025 via Ember's monthly CEA pipeline.

Q2: Is the data fully corrected, and are there regulatory ambiguities?

The data reflects operational reality with documented, auditable corrections. Raw transcription anomalies (such as negative deficit rows) are preserved in the database for audit integrity while cleanly clamped in visual charts. Legitimate differences between Grid-India telemetry and CEA annual figures stem from SCADA instantaneous transducers vs post-facto Special Energy Meter (SEM) billing settlements.

Q3: What official APIs or datasets are available in India?

  • CEA Data API Portal (cea.nic.in): Programmatic access to installed capacity, power supply position, and generation statistics.
  • National Power Portal (npp.gov.in): Centralized repository maintained by CEA and MoP for daily station generation and regional load summaries.
  • NITI Aayog ICED (iced.niti.gov.in): India Climate and Energy Dashboard with clean downloads of state-level load curves and generation time-series.
  • Vidyut Pravah (vidyutpravah.in): Near real-time operational portal operated by Grid-India with 15-minute dispatch and price updates.
  • Open Government Data (data.gov.in): Official REST API for statutory releases.
  • Ember Climate API: Free, open REST API providing monthly generation by fuel type for India (integrated into Tab 4).
